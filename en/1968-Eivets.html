Second stage: Young Stevie Wonder

EIVETS REDNOW...ALFIE

1968

An album of pop songs—centered on the sound of the harmonica performed by a person multi-instrumentalist much like Stevie Wonder had been since the start of his career. Wonder was experiencing friction with Motown due to the label’s rigid artistic control, so he released this record under the name -"Eivets Rednow"- curiously his name read backwards from right to left.

Stevie hosts some of the spirit of Burt Bacharach and Toots Thielemans, but his chords and arrangements for the chromatic harmonica venture into broader territory, showcasing his virtuosity on the instrument and his musical maturity. All of this, once again, with production by Henry Cosby and songwriting contributions on some tracks by Stevie Wonder.
