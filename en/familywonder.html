WONDER FAMILY

The family has always been an important pillar in Stevie Wonder's life. The protection he felt from his mother -Mama Lula- towards him and his siblings, wove invisible threads in his mind that have served to create a warm home with each of his offspring.

Father of 9 children through different women with whom he has been involved, except for his first marriage Syreeta Wright, with whom he was married from 1970 to 1972, with all his other relationships he has had descendants, many of whom have followed paths related to the world of art, music, or fashion.

Aisha Morris

Born on February 2, 1975, Aisha Morris was his first daughter, the result of a relationship with the secretary at Motown Yolanda Simmons. She was the inspiration that motivated the creation of the song “Isn’t She Lovely?”- on the 1976 album Songs In The Key Of Life. She is a singer and has participated in several songs with Stevie Wonder, including “How Will I Know” a duet with his father for which they were nominated for a Grammy. She also accompanied him on various tours and performances around the world, including his Live At Last and served as musical director of KJLH, the radio station is owned by Wonder. In her personal life, she is the mother of a son named Miles.

Keita Morris

Keita Morris, was his second child with Yolanda Simmons. Born on April 17, 1977. Although he does not lead as active a public life as his sister, he also works in the music world as a producer and DJ under the name "DJ Jersey Wonder," founded the label Stay Gold in New York, and is the father of Asmar and Lula.

Mumtaz Morris

Mumtaz Morris was his third child from his relationship with the vocalist Melody McCulley. He was born on October 13, 1983, and after a period of time involved in sports such as American football and basketball, he pursued a musical career, performing with his father and releasing a single with Snoop Dogg, "Do It Well." He also participated alongside four of his siblings in the Wonder's song "Where Is Our Love Song",And currently he seems to work as an insurance expert while being the father of five children with his wife Candice.

Sophia and Kwame

Stevie Wonder became a father again as a result of a relationship with a woman whose identity he did not reveal, although many point to Sheryo Stone.
Sophia Morris was born in July 1985. In honor of her father and his work with blind children, she partnered with Boodaddy Diamonds to design a necklace that spells out the word "love" in Braille. She produces content for Global Citizen Live and was instrumental in organizing World Day for African and Afro-descendant Culture in San José, Costa Rica, serving as an ambassador to UNESCO. She has a very close relationship with her father, whom she calls her "best friend."
For his part Kwame Morris was born on August 6, 1988, in Sydney, Australia, where he lived until he was 12. He then moved to Los Angeles and later to Atlanta, where he studied sociology, communication, and art at Morehouse College. He has worked as a production coordinator and model, even appearing in Vogue Italia. Inspired by his father's impressive eyewear collection, he founded Yagan Stone Eyewear. The company sells eyewear adorned with gemstones, handcrafted in Italy. Kwame also composes and produces music and has collaborated with artists such as TrapMoneyBenny, FNZ, and Edwin Arzu. He became a father to Kenzo in 2023 with his partner, Akira Egawa.

Kailand and Mandla

In 2001 he married the fashion designer Kai Millard Morris, his second wife, the first was Syreeta Wright, with whom he had no children. The marriage ended in divorce years later, specifically in 2012. Both parents maintained a good relationship, united by their love for their two children, Kailand and Mandla, whom they strongly influenced in their professional paths.
Kailand Morris was born on September 1, 2001. He is Stevie Wonder's sixth child. He grew up in Calabasas and played drums on Stevie Wonder's tours from a very young age. His mother's strong influence led him to redirect his life towards fashion, working for Dolce & Gabbana and launching his own fashion label, House of K.O.M., in 2019. He participated with Stevie Wonder at the 2025 Met Gala and also shares his father's passion for philanthropy, designing two lines of T-shirts for the Black Lives Matter movement.
Mandla Morris was born on May 13, 2005, curiously the same day as her father's birthday. At 13 years old, He debuted as a contestant on Dancing with the Stars: Juniors, and like his brother, he is a fashion designer inspired by the late Virgil Abloh. He had a cameo in the Oscar-winning film "A Star Is Born" as the pastor's son, and announced on social media his intention to pursue a career in music production.

Zaiah and Nyah

In 2017 he married his current wife Tomeeka Robyn Bracy. They started the relationship years ago, and both children were present at the wedding celebration.
Zaiah Morris is Stevie Wonder's eighth child. Born in 2013, she shows great interest in music. In 2020, her voice appeared on Stevie's song "Where Is Our Love Song," along with four of her siblings. Years later, in 2022, she sang "The Christmas Song" with her father and younger sister, Nyah, at the House Full of Toys benefit concert, and she has been seen dancing confidently with her sister with the father's Christmas song "One Little Christmas Tree."
Niah Morris is Stevie Wonder's ninth and youngest child. Born on December 17, 2014, her name means "purpose," one of the seven principles of Kwanzaa, an annual celebration of African-American culture. As mentioned earlier, she participated in her father's song "Where Is Our Love Song," and it wouldn't be surprising if she later pursued a career in the performing arts and music.
