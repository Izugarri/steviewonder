A man, a musician, an artist

A GENIUS STEVIE WONDER

   Original Spanish

Genuine, authentic, generous and supportive..., the genius with more magic of the music of our time. Virtuous on the keyboards, powerful with his voice and with an exquisite sensitivity conveying emotions flowing through delicious compositions performed masterfully. Stevie Wonder is much more than a musician, is a reference to the music of the 20th-21st century and point of inspiration for new generations create music with soul. Through this web we only try to announce the musical excellences of this man, Stevie Wonder. In his long musical trajectory he has left one to us without end of songs and compositions.

Pioneer and visionary of music, Stevie Wonder is the first artist to put the single and album simultaneously at number one on the charts of sale, besides being the youngest person to get it. He is also the first artist in history to reach at the same time the number 1 four different Billboard charts with a song. He has been creating music for over 60 years, which has been rewarded with numerous number one hits. A good number of videos that we have been compiling both alone and accompanied by great artists. In the same way a privileged place in the most important lists of the whole world, like the Billboard, 25 awards Grammy and especially the respect of the industry of the musician and the public in general.


New times began for Stevie Wonder in this new decade. Several songs advanced us in these post-pandemic years; "Where Is Our Love Song", "Can't Put It In the Hands of Fate", "The Living Killing Life", "This I Know" and "Can We Fix Our Nation’s Broken Heart". To these we must add the duet with Elton John "Finish Line", his participation with The Rolling Stone, Gray Clark Jr., Cher...

But above all, what we all hope for is to obtain the greatest of the gifts that we all desire, the publication of his new musical project that will surely soak us in authentic magic, which only a genius like Stevie Wonder is capable of providing us.
