Video

(You're My) Dream Come True
"(You're My) Dream Come True" is one of the songs that Stevie Wonder -together with the group The temptations- contributed as a tribute to Motown founder Berry Gordy, jr. The name of that tribute album was "The Music, The Magic, The Memories Of Motown (A Tribute To Berry Gordy)" and it was published in 1995.
